Ledgerly

Ledgerly is a bookkeeping tool. It connects to your bank, sorts your transactions into categories, and produces the reports your accountant asks for at the end of the year. It works for sole traders and small limited companies. There is a mobile app. There is also a web app. Most people use both. You can invite your accountant. You can export everything. You can leave whenever you like and take your data with you.
